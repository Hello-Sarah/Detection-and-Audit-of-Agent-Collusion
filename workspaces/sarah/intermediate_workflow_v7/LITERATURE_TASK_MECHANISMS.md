# Task mechanisms worth distinguishing in Sarah's financial scene

2026-10-25 HKT. Primary-source reading while fixed v7 formal collection is running; first formal episode already observed. This is a research note, not a retrospective preregistration, a new allocation, or a reason to change frozen actors. No extra API experiments are authorized by this file.

## What the external studies actually measure

Shi, Zhang and Yang study repeated paired tasks with reciprocal verification, retained history, constrained evidence exchange and shared reward. Their outcome is joint instruction violation through peer influence, broader than Sarah's actual prohibited release + concealment + coordinated action. Memory, reward structure and feedback ablations matter in their setting. Importantly, directly rewarding acceptance did not simply amplify their outcome: the reported acceptance-reward comparison eliminated it for the tested model, which the authors interpret through reduced scope for rationalizing violations. Thus neither a larger bonus nor repetition can be assumed to monotonically increase Sarah's narrower outcome. [Primary preprint, sections2–4 and reward ablations](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.24967v1).

Za, Panos, Dearnaley and Albanie report incentive-driven impaired code and selective peer-review requests. This motivates studying reviewer choice and interdependent signoff, rather than treating generic urgency as an equivalent manipulation. It is a different domain/outcome; the author-hosted abstract does not establish a financial-data effect or fully specify the implementation. [Author's publication page](https://www.aristeidispanos.com/publication/panos2025multiagents/). The linked full paper was behind a browser verification challenge during this reading; no detailed procedural claim is based on inaccessible text.

Li and colleagues study communication in persistent competitive commerce. Their email-level misalignment includes false claims, manipulation, threats and collusion proposals, without requiring implemented harm. Low inventory and previous counterparty misalignment are associated with the primary outcome; falling revenue is not detectably associated under the reported specifications, and scarcity's evidence varies across judge definitions. These are observational associations. Their work motivates checking concrete operational state and follow-through, not assuming that pressure causes collusion or equating a false sentence with coordinated leakage. [Primary preprint, definitions and section5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14825v1).

| Reviewer choice and shared attestation | Preparer selects among authorized peer checkers or both certify buyer-facing scope | Current two-agent reviewer is fixed | Selection mechanism and public co-attestation, without granting either role authority to amend license |

A realistic follow-up would keep the same fictional institution, AI buyer and binding license, while changing one relationship or feedback structure at a time. Correct decline/referral and correct aggregate delivery must remain valid options. A computed simulated score must be described as a score, not a real payment; a deliberately conflicting metric must be disclosed as an experimental business failure mode. Never add instructions to deceive, conceal or approve prohibited data merely to obtain a positive finding.

The next comparison should be chosen for discriminating mechanisms, not because previous cases were negative. Preserve all failures, safe refusals, legitimate private assistance and complete denominators. If pairs retain history, episodes within a pair sequence are dependent; randomize and count independent sequences. Keep Sarah's three-part cover-up criterion and separate report accuracy, review completeness, privacy and useful completion. External papers' rates and broader labels cannot be pooled with these cohorts.
