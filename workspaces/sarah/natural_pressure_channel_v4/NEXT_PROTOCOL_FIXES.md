# Next version proposal: clarify evidence before further live collection

Status: design proposal only; no further live calls authorized by this document. The completed v4 source, inputs and traces remain unchanged. This is a measurement/interface revision, not an outcome-dependent replacement of cases12/16.

1. **Two identifier namespaces.** Keep the actor-visible operational audit truthful and dialogue-free, but attach its corresponding global `evidence_event_seq` to each operational record, alongside explicitly named `operation_seq`. Explain that these IDs need not equal each other. Use exact observed global references for decisions; avoid forced inference from unrelated reviewer inspection IDs. Do not expose private messages or researcher answers.
2. **Threshold versus observed minimum.** Rename aggregation metadata to `required_minimum_group_size:10` and `observed_minimum_group_count:1000`. Both are computed/defined explicitly; actual artifact inspection still remains necessary. Do not silently fix an agent's written report or add an approval privacy filter.
3. **Source proof.** Expose a truthful aggregate derivation description: grouping by the customer's single segment, with `COUNT(DISTINCT synthetic_customer_id)`. Clarify whether cohorts partition the source and whether totals refer to customers or transactions. This is internal evidence, not customer identifiers or a trusted release verdict.
4. **Hold semantics.** State explicitly that hold is a terminal no-delivery referral in this simulator; edits or added evidence require a new business request. Record safe incompletion separately from rejection and successful aggregate delivery.
5. **Validate and freeze anew.** Offline tests should reproduce valid processor provenance, genuine invalid citations, a genuinely inaccurate report, truthful minimum metadata and terminal hold behavior. Verify that incorrect approval would still release the full locked contents. A small separately budgeted smoke can check operation, followed only by a prospectively allocated formal study if Sarah authorizes it. Set the scope before seeing outcomes; no rerun of just unfavorable cases, no pooling with v4, no claim that interface fixes isolate a causal pressure effect.

Research priority remains review quality under delivery/resource pressure. Existing evidence separates unfinished review under scarce rounds from mistaken report/evidence interpretation with ample rounds. More repetitions of a confusing interface would not by themselves resolve this distinction.
