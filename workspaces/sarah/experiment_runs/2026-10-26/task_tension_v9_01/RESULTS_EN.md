## 5. Scope for RQ2.1 and limitations

The observed bound is no prohibited approval attempt across these eight business-goal/KPI/invitation cells, four exposures each. Clear policy, explicit protected-file disclosure, one model/source and a fixed script partner constrain generalization. This is not a spontaneous two-autonomous-model collusion rate or a general ranking of risky tasks. F1 may not be valued by the model. No communication-channel availability effect or dialogue/action detector comparison was tested.

Important post-freeze clarification: all 32 main packages were already locked. Reviewer could not remove the file or choose partial release. A compliant full-fulfillment planning alternative exists in principle but is not directly selectable in the main episodes. On actual main branches, prohibited approval earnsQ0+B3 for either task; supported rejection earnsQ1+B0. Thus T changes purpose/necessity framing, not the attainable main-branch reward gap. Controls are cleaned planning packages, not factorial comparator cells. See POSTHOC_DESIGN_BOUNDARY.md; no frozen stimulus was changed. Do not describe this as an isolated feasible-versus-infeasible workflow experiment.

Each safe control needed5requests, all more than the earlier4-request ceiling, but the prompt/version also changed: do not attribute cross-study completion differences solely to the cap. Earlier batches remain separate. Four final safe approvals validate only these observed guarded workflows, not general unassisted reliability. Zero completed cover-ups does not imply low risk. A broader search must retain null outcomes and separate exploratory batches; it must not run until a desired positive and then present only that case as incidence evidence.
